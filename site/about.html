Company and platform information

About AUTOLINK

AUTOLINK is a global B2B vehicle sourcing information platform connecting international automotive buyers with suppliers in China.

What the platform does

We help importers, dealers, traders and other buyers research Chinese vehicle brands and models, review published supplier information and available public listings, and submit sourcing inquiries.

  • Vehicle brands and model specifications
  • Reviewed supplier profiles and public vehicle listings
  • Buyer sourcing requests and inquiries
  • Platform membership and digital information services

The platform's role and limits

AUTOLINK provides digital platform and membership services. It does not directly sell the vehicles shown as model references, collect vehicle purchase payments or deposits, act as transaction escrow, arrange international vehicle shipping, provide customs clearance, or guarantee third-party transactions.

A model reference is not a stock offer. Specifications, images, inventory, pricing, export conditions and delivery arrangements must be confirmed with the relevant supplier or independent service provider. Destination-country import and registration rules require separate verification.

Our official website is https://autolinkglobal.com/. For platform questions, use the official support page.

Frequently asked questions

What does AUTOLINK do?

AUTOLINK connects international automotive buyers with Chinese vehicle suppliers through model information, published supplier listings and sourcing inquiries.

Does AUTOLINK provide international vehicle shipping or customs clearance?

No. AUTOLINK does not directly provide vehicle shipping, customs clearance or freight forwarding. The relevant parties or independent providers arrange those services separately.

Does AUTOLINK sell vehicles directly?

AUTOLINK operates as a B2B information and sourcing platform. A model shown for research is not an offer of stock from AUTOLINK. Vehicle transactions are handled by the relevant counterparties.

What does AUTOLINK charge for?

Applicable platform memberships and digital information services may have fees shown in the current plan and membership terms. Vehicle purchases and related services are arranged separately by the relevant counterparties.
