MODEL RESEARCH

Identify the China-market version before discussing supply.

A familiar international badge can cover a different trim, battery, engine or model year in China. Start with the exact model and configuration in AUTOLINK's reference catalogue. For example, Geely Monjaro / Xingyue L, BYD Qin L and Changan CS75 PLUS have distinct source records. These links are research references and do not say that any of these vehicles can currently be supplied to a Middle East country.

Record the production year, source trim, engine or battery version, seating, steering side and test cycle for any published range figure. Ask the seller to reconcile the actual vehicle documents with the reference before relying on the specification.

SUPPLY EVIDENCE

A catalogue model and an available vehicle are different records.

Open the public supplier vehicle directory to see only listings that have passed the platform's publication gate. A brand page, model photo or configuration table is not a stock declaration. If the directory has no matching listing, submit a requirement and wait for a real supplier to respond; no price, FOB term or delivery date should be inferred from the reference page.

For a proposed listing, request the seller's company identity, the vehicle identification details that can be shared for verification, current stock evidence, and the exact condition and quantity. Manufacturer authorization is a separate claim that needs separate evidence.

DESTINATION CHECK

Check the actual destination country and vehicle independently.

The Middle East is not one import jurisdiction. Registration, vehicle age, steering position, technical approval, customs paperwork, taxes and product requirements can differ by destination and may change. Before placing an order, the importer should confirm the current rules with its local competent authority and qualified customs or compliance advisers. AUTOLINK does not certify that a catalogue vehicle is eligible for import or registration in any country.

Ask which language the vehicle interface, owner material and service documentation support, and whether charging hardware or fuel specification suits the destination. These are questions for the actual trim and intended country, not claims about every model in a series.

INQUIRY CHECKLIST

Make supplier quotations comparable.

Send the model ID and trim, desired quantity, destination country and port, requested steering side, vehicle condition and required documents. Request a written quotation that identifies currency, validity period, origin port, lead time, and any trade term actually offered. A term such as FOB must include a named place and clearly defined responsibilities; the platform does not publish an unverified FOB price for a reference model.

Compare responses on the same vehicle, condition, quantity, delivery basis and document scope. Confirm payment, transport and any inspection terms directly in the parties' agreement. AUTOLINK provides discovery and inquiry tools; it does not hold vehicle purchase funds or guarantee completion of a trade.
