MARKET CONTEXT

Start with the country, not a continent-wide import promise.

Africa includes markets with different steering, inspection, registration and documentation requirements. A supplier's ability to source a model does not establish its eligibility in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Morocco or another destination. The importer should confirm current rules for the particular vehicle with the destination authority and a qualified local adviser before committing to a purchase.

The U.S. Commercial Service's Nigeria automotive overview identifies China as a leading source of new vehicles and parts, while noting that incomplete data makes the total vehicle market hard to quantify. That is a reason to research actual buyer requirements, not evidence that every China-market trim has Nigerian approval. For Kenya, the Kenya Revenue Authority's vehicle import guidance calls out right-hand drive and roadworthiness inspection; verify the latest applicable rule before proposing a vehicle.

MODEL REFERENCE

Record the trim, steering side and source year.

Use the model reference directory to identify a China-market vehicle, then check its source configuration. The name used abroad may refer to a different engine, battery, equipment package or year. A published photo and specification table describe a reference configuration; they do not prove an export-ready right-hand-drive variant exists.

For a shortlist, record model ID, exact trim, model and first-registration years where relevant, powertrain, range test cycle if electric, steering side, and the destination country's document requirements. Do not copy a China-market test-cycle figure into a destination-market claim without stating its test basis.

PUBLIC SUPPLY

Confirm an approved listing and the supplier's own evidence.

Review public supplier vehicles separately from the reference catalogue. If there is no approved listing for the exact model, the page cannot promise stock, price or delivery. Ask the supplier to substantiate vehicle identity, quantity, condition, export documents and the company responsible for the sale. Company verification and vehicle review do not establish brand-dealer authorization; any authorization claim must be checked for the named supplier, brand, territory, scope and validity period.

BUYER INQUIRY

State the destination and a comparable specification.

Send the model reference, quantity, destination country and port, required steering side, trim, condition and document list. Ask the seller to identify the actual available vehicle and state currency, quote validity, origin port, trade term and lead time in its own written offer. Do not infer an FOB price from a model page. AUTOLINK connects buyers with supplier information and purchase requests; it does not provide customs clearance, shipping or vehicle-purchase escrow.
