COMPANY PROFILE

What does a supplier profile include?

Start with the company profile. Understand your partner step by step.

  • Company name and registration region
  • Business operating location
  • Company type and business scope
  • Declared main automotive brands
  • Public vehicle listings and supply information
  • Business information review status
  • Public contact information and inquiry channels

Information depends on what the company has submitted and is permitted to publish. Only public information allowed by access controls is displayed.

How are business review statuses presented?

Under its current review rules, the platform may request business registration, premises information and relevant supporting documents.

After review, the displayed status must reflect the actual outcome. An unreviewed business must not be labelled a verified supplier.

REVIEW STATUS

Four statuses. Four different meanings.

01

Account enabled

The account may use the features granted to it. This does not mean that the business or its listings have passed review.

02

Information submitted

Information has been submitted or is awaiting review. Submission is not approval.

03

Business information reviewed

The corresponding company information has passed the current platform review process. Check the status actually shown.

04

Specific listing reviewed

A separate review of a particular vehicle or supply offer. Business review does not automatically approve a listing.

A review helps you understand a business. It does not replace transaction checks.

A platform review of business information does not mean any of the following certifications or guarantees:

  • Government certification
  • Official manufacturer authorization
  • Inventory or supply guarantee
  • Transaction performance guarantee

Before a formal transaction, buyers should independently verify the supplier’s identity, vehicle source and contract conditions.
