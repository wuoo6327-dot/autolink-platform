Declared brands do not mean manufacturer authorization.

A supplier’s main brands describe its self-declared business or supply scope. They do not automatically indicate official manufacturer authorization. A specific authorization status may be displayed only when the platform has checked the relevant documents and publication is permitted.

BEFORE YOUR INQUIRY

Questions to take into your sourcing conversation

  • 01Production year and exact version
  • 02Left-hand or right-hand drive
  • 03Powertrain and energy type
  • 04Actual equipment and technical specifications
  • 05New, used or other vehicle condition
  • 06In stock, sourced on demand or pre-order
  • 07Minimum quantity and delivery lead time
  • 08Vehicle import requirements in the destination country
