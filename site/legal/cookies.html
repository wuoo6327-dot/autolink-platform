Cookie Policy

Last updated: To be confirmed · Version 1.0

This policy describes the browser storage used by AUTOLINK for sign-in, language, privacy choices and optional visit statistics. The current site uses local storage rather than setting its own browser cookies.

1. Necessary storage

Sign-in session, language and privacy-choice records are necessary for the site to work. They cannot be turned off in the preference panel, although a user can clear them in browser settings; doing so may sign the user out or reset preferences.

2. Optional analytics

If a user chooses visit statistics, AUTOLINK may create a short-lived anonymous visit identifier for its own first-party statistics. This identifier is removed when consent is withdrawn. No Google Analytics or Meta Pixel script was found in the current site build.

3. Marketing storage

AUTOLINK does not currently run marketing cookies or pixels in the reviewed build. Marketing preferences are stored only as a choice for any future implementation; a marketing tool would require a policy update and any legally required consent before activation.

4. Managing preferences

Use the Cookie Settings control on the site to accept all, reject optional categories or save a custom choice. Browser settings can also clear local storage. Withdrawal does not change processing that lawfully occurred before it.

5. Storage inventory

AUTOLINK currently uses autolink-language for language choice, autolink-privacy-v1 for consent choices (up to 180 days), autolink-business-session for a remembered login (up to 30 minutes), and autolink-anonymous-visit-v1 for consented visit statistics (up to 24 hours). Necessary service-side session hashes also expire after 30 minutes.

6. Contact

Questions about storage or privacy can be sent to To be confirmed.

AUTOLINK · 法律与隐私

Cookie 政策

最后更新：待确认 · 版本 1.0

本政策说明 AUTOLINK 如何使用 Cookie、本地存储及类似技术。Cookie 是网站存储在用户设备上的小型数据文件，用于识别会话、记住偏好、保障安全和分析网站运行情况。

1. 必要 Cookie

必要 Cookie 用于实现账号登录、身份认证、安全校验、语言设置、隐私选择和核心页面功能。这类 Cookie 对网站正常运行是必要的，因此通常无法通过 Cookie 面板关闭，但用户可通过浏览器设置删除或阻止 Cookie；这可能导致部分功能无法使用。

2. 分析 Cookie

在获得用户同意后，平台可能使用分析 Cookie 了解页面访问量、访问来源、功能使用情况、性能和错误，以改进网站体验。若平台使用 Google Analytics 或其他统计服务，应在本页面列出实际供应商、Cookie 名称、用途和保存期限。

3. 营销 Cookie

若未来使用广告投放、再营销或跨站追踪技术，平台将在启用前更新本政策，并通过 Cookie 面板取得适用法律要求的同意。若当前未使用营销 Cookie，页面应明确写“当前未启用营销 Cookie”，不要列出不存在的工具。

4. Cookie 管理

用户可通过网站的“Cookie 设置”随时调整非必要 Cookie 偏好。撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意进行处理的合法性。用户也可通过浏览器设置管理或删除 Cookie。

5. 当前存储清单

当前网站使用本地存储：autolink-language 保存语言偏好；autolink-privacy-v1 保存隐私选择，最长 180 天；autolink-business-session 在用户选择保持登录时保存会话，最长 30 分钟；autolink-anonymous-visit-v1 仅在允许访问统计后生成，最长 24 小时。当前页面构建中没有 Google Analytics 或 Meta Pixel，未启用营销 Cookie。

6. 联系方式

有关 Cookie 或隐私设置的问题，请联系：待确认。
