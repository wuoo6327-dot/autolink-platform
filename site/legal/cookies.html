Cookie Policy

Last updated: To be confirmed · Version 1.0

This policy describes the browser storage used by AUTOLINK for sign-in, language, privacy choices and optional visit statistics. The site uses a necessary HttpOnly sign-in Cookie together with browser local storage for preferences.

1. Necessary storage

Sign-in session, language and privacy-choice records are necessary for the site to work. They cannot be turned off in the preference panel, although a user can clear them in browser settings; doing so may sign the user out or reset preferences.

2. Optional analytics

If a user chooses visit statistics, AUTOLINK may create a short-lived anonymous visit identifier for its own first-party statistics. This identifier is removed when consent is withdrawn. The current site does not enable Google Analytics or Meta Pixel.

3. Marketing storage

AUTOLINK does not currently run marketing cookies or pixels. Marketing preferences are stored only as a choice for any future implementation; a marketing tool would require a policy update and any legally required consent before activation.

4. Managing preferences

Use the Cookie Settings control on the site to accept all, reject optional categories or save a custom choice. Browser settings can also clear Cookies and local storage. Withdrawal does not change processing that lawfully occurred before it.

5. Storage inventory

The autolink_session Cookie authenticates sign-in. In production it is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax; normal server sessions expire after 30 minutes, while Keep me signed in sessions last up to 7 days. autolink-language (and legacy autolink_language) stores language choice; autolink-privacy-v1 stores consent choices for up to 180 days; autolink-anonymous-visit-v1 is used only for consented visit statistics for up to 24 hours. Browser storage also holds comparison selections and a non-secret autolink-cookie-session sign-in marker. The older autolink-business-session token is read only for login compatibility; it is not the current Cookie login mechanism.

Questions about storage or privacy can be sent to To be confirmed.

AUTOLINK · 法律与隐私

Cookie 政策

最后更新：待确认 · 版本 1.0

本政策说明 AUTOLINK 如何使用 Cookie、本地存储及类似技术。Cookie 是网站存储在用户设备上的小型数据文件，用于识别会话、记住偏好、保障安全和分析网站运行情况。

1. 必要 Cookie

必要 Cookie 用于实现账号登录、身份认证、安全校验、语言设置、隐私选择和核心页面功能。这类 Cookie 对网站正常运行是必要的，因此通常无法通过 Cookie 面板关闭，但用户可通过浏览器设置删除或阻止 Cookie；这可能导致部分功能无法使用。

2. 分析 Cookie

允许访问统计后，网站可保存最长 24 小时的第一方匿名访问标识；撤回统计选择时会移除该标识。当前未启用 Google Analytics 或 Meta Pixel。

3. 营销 Cookie

当前未启用营销 Cookie 或广告像素。营销偏好仅保存用户的选择；如将来启用相关工具，需要先更新政策并取得适用法律要求的同意。

4. Cookie 管理

用户可通过网站的“Cookie 设置”随时调整非必要 Cookie 偏好。撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意进行处理的合法性。用户也可通过浏览器设置管理或删除 Cookie。

5. 当前存储清单

autolink_session Cookie 用于登录认证，生产环境使用 HttpOnly、Secure 和 SameSite=Lax。普通登录的服务端会话为 30 分钟；选择保持登录时最长 7 天。autolink-language（兼容旧键 autolink_language）保存语言；autolink-privacy-v1 保存隐私选择，最长 180 天；autolink-anonymous-visit-v1 仅在允许统计后保存，最长 24 小时。本地还保存车型对比选择和不含凭证的 autolink-cookie-session 登录提示标记。旧 autolink-business-session 仅用于兼容旧登录凭证，不是当前 Cookie 登录机制。

6. 联系方式

有关 Cookie 或隐私设置的问题，请联系：待确认。
