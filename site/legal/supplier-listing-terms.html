1. Service and eligibility

Only a supplier whose company and specific vehicle listing have passed AUTOLINK review may buy this service. One SKC means one supplier's one brand, model and model year; configurations or colours within that SKC are not charged separately. The fee buys public display of that reviewed SKC for one quarter. It is not a vehicle purchase payment, deposit, manufacturer authorisation, export certification or guarantee of a sale. Company review, brand authorisation review and vehicle listing review remain separate.

2. Price, period and renewal

The first quarter is USD 40 per SKC, the second is USD 140, and each later quarter is USD 200. The server calculates the applicable amount from paid service history; the amount and currency shown before Checkout govern that order. Each payment covers three months, starting when payment is confirmed or after the existing paid period ends, as applicable. Renewal is initiated by the supplier. There is no automatic renewal or automatic charge.

3. Payment and publication

Company and vehicle review must be complete before payment. After Stripe confirms payment, an eligible listing whose reviewed details have not changed is published on the same day. If its details have changed or it no longer meets public listing rules, AUTOLINK does not publish it without review. Contact support for review or refund handling. Payment does not itself verify stock, price, manufacturer authorisation or the outcome of a vehicle transaction.

4. Changes, expiry and suspension

The supplier must keep stock, photos, pricing and other material information accurate. AUTOLINK may require a new review or pause public display after a material change. At expiry or following a full refund, the listing ceases public display, while necessary order and audit records may be retained. If the supplier provides false information, changes a listing without required review, or loses its review qualification, AUTOLINK pauses display and conducts a manual review. Where the reason is not attributable to AUTOLINK, a refund is generally not given, subject to mandatory law.

5. Refunds and disputes

A refund may be considered after verification of a duplicate charge, failure to activate after payment for a reason attributable to AUTOLINK, a substantial platform outage that prevents display for an extended period, or where applicable law requires it. A normally activated service or a listing withdrawn by the supplier is generally not refundable, subject to mandatory law. Partial refunds, chargebacks and disputes require manual review; they do not automatically extend or restore a listing. Submit a refund request through AUTOLINK support. AUTOLINK acknowledges receipt within three business days and gives a decision within ten business days after receiving complete information. The payment channel determines when a refund reaches the original payment method.

6. Receipts and business invoices

A Stripe payment receipt confirms the payment. A supplier needing a business invoice for the listing service may request one through AUTOLINK support, quoting the paid order number and its legal company name, business registration or tax identifier if applicable, billing address and delivery email. AUTOLINK verifies the paid order and refund status before issuing a document from the operating company. A refund or dispute may require the invoice and accounting records to be corrected. A Hong Kong business invoice is not a Mainland China VAT special invoice; confirm any local tax-document requirement before paying.

7. Limits and contact

AUTOLINK provides information display and business introductions. The supplier and buyer confirm vehicle price, stock, delivery, export, import and contract responsibilities independently. This service does not guarantee views, enquiries, sales or search ranking. Questions, invoice requests and refund requests may be submitted through AUTOLINK support.
