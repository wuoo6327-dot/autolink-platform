AUTOLINK · LEGAL

Account Deletion

Proposed effective date: 17 October 2026 · Version 1.0

AUTOLINK users may request deletion of their platform account. We may verify the requester's identity before acting to protect the account.

1. How to delete an account

Sign in, use the account action on this page and confirm to close the account. The platform owner and the last active administrator cannot close their accounts through this action. If you cannot sign in, request assistance at chemerimy@gmail.com or through the support form and complete reasonable identity checks.

2. What happens after deletion

The account is marked closed and all active sessions end. This action does not erase the database or historical enquiries, quotes and audit records. Requests to erase retained personal information may be sent through the same contact channels and require identity verification. The categories, retention periods and removal method still require legal and technical review; the 1–3 business day commitment below covers account closure only.

3. Third-party authorization

Deleting an AUTOLINK account does not delete a Google or other provider account. Users may separately revoke AUTOLINK authorization in their provider's account settings.

4. Processing time

We acknowledge a complete, verifiable request and close the account within 1–3 business days after identity verification, unless further review is needed. Closure revokes access and sessions; it is not a promise that all records or backups are physically erased within that period. Records that must be retained are deleted or anonymized when the requirement ends.

5. Contact

Account deletion and data requests: chemerimy@gmail.com or the support form; phone +852 3460 5868.

AUTOLINK · 法律与隐私

账号删除

拟生效日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日 · 版本 1.0

AUTOLINK 用户可以申请删除自己的平台账户。为了保护账户安全，平台可能在处理删除请求前要求用户重新登录、完成邮箱/手机验证或提供其他合理身份验证。

1. 删除方式

1. 使用注册账号登录 AUTOLINK，打开《账号删除》页面，使用页面中的账号关闭入口，再次确认后关闭账号并终止会话。平台所有者及最后一个有效管理员不能通过此入口关闭账号。2. 无法登录时，可通过客服邮箱或站内支持表单申请删除，并完成合理身份核验。

2. 删除后将发生什么

  • 账户将被关闭，用户将无法继续登录或使用需要账户的功能；
  • 此入口将账号标记为已关闭，不会清空数据库、历史询盘、报价或审计记录；
  • 需要进一步删除保留的个人信息时，可通过本页列出的邮箱或站内支持表单申请，并完成身份核验。具体资料类别、留存期限和清理方式仍待法律与技术审查；1–3 个工作日承诺仅适用于账号关闭；
  • 经匿名化后无法再识别具体用户的数据，可能继续用于统计、安全和产品改进。

3. 第三方登录授权

删除 AUTOLINK 账户不会自动删除用户在 Google 平台的账号。用户可同时前往相应第三方账户安全设置撤销对 AUTOLINK 的授权。

4. 处理期限

平台在完成身份核验并收到完整申请后，原则上 1–3 个工作日内确认受理并关闭账号；需进一步核查的，将告知处理进度。账号关闭会撤销登录与会话，不代表全部业务记录和备份在该期限内物理清除。若部分数据因财税、交易、诉讼、安全、反欺诈或其他法定义务需要继续保存，平台将在必要期限届满后再依法删除或匿名化。

5. 联系方式

账号删除与数据请求邮箱：chemerimy@gmail.com；亦可使用站内支持表单；电话：+852 3460 5868。
