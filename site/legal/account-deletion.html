Account Deletion

Last updated: To be confirmed · Version 1.0

AUTOLINK users may request deletion of their platform account. We may verify the requester's identity before acting to protect the account.

1. How to delete an account

Sign in, open the Privacy Policy from the site footer and choose Delete my AUTOLINK account in the account-data section, then confirm. If you cannot sign in, request assistance at To be confirmed and complete reasonable identity checks.

2. What happens after deletion

The account is closed and access ends. Profile and other nonessential content are removed or unpublished. Records needed for law, security, tax or an unresolved transaction or dispute may be kept only as necessary; irreversibly anonymized statistics may remain.

3. Third-party authorization

Deleting an AUTOLINK account does not delete a Google or other provider account. Users may separately revoke AUTOLINK authorization in their provider's account settings.

4. Processing time

Verified requests are handled within To be confirmed, unless applicable law requires another period. Records that must be retained are deleted or anonymized when that requirement ends.

5. Contact

Account deletion and data requests: To be confirmed.

账号删除

最后更新：待确认 · 版本 1.0

AUTOLINK 用户可以申请删除自己的平台账户。为了保护账户安全，平台可能在处理删除请求前要求用户重新登录、完成邮箱/手机验证或提供其他合理身份验证。

1. 删除方式

1. 使用注册账号登录 AUTOLINK，打开《账号删除》页面，点击“删除我的 AUTOLINK 账号”，再次确认后立即删除。2. 无法登录时，可向待确认申请删除，并完成合理身份核验。

2. 删除后将发生什么

  • 账户将被关闭，用户将无法继续登录或使用需要账户的功能；
  • 公开企业资料、个人资料和非必要内容将按系统规则删除或取消公开；
  • 与其他用户已产生的必要交易、争议、安全或审计记录，可能根据法律义务和合理业务需要继续保留；
  • 经匿名化后无法再识别具体用户的数据，可能继续用于统计、安全和产品改进。

3. 第三方登录授权

删除 AUTOLINK 账户不会自动删除用户在 Google 平台的账号。用户可同时前往相应第三方账户安全设置撤销对 AUTOLINK 的授权。

4. 处理期限

平台将在收到完整且可验证的删除申请后，在待确认内进行处理，适用法律另有规定的除外。若部分数据因财税、交易、诉讼、安全、反欺诈或其他法定义务需要继续保存，平台将在必要期限届满后再依法删除或匿名化。

5. 联系方式

账号删除与数据请求邮箱：待确认。
