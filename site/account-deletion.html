Delete your AUTOLINK account
删除 AUTOLINK 账号

You can remove your AUTOLINK business account and the Google, LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram sign-in data linked to it. / 你可以删除 AUTOLINK 商家账号及其关联的 Google、LinkedIn、Facebook 或 Instagram 登录资料。

How to delete / 删除步骤

  1. Sign in at AUTOLINK login with the same account you used to register. / 使用注册时的账号登录 AUTOLINK。
  2. Open Privacy Policy and choose “Delete my AUTOLINK account” near the social account section. / 打开“隐私政策”，在社交账号说明处点击“删除我的 AUTOLINK 账号”。
  3. Confirm the deletion. Your AUTOLINK profile, linked provider identifiers and active sessions will be removed. / 确认删除后，AUTOLINK 将移除你的资料、关联平台标识和有效登录会话。

What this does not delete / 不会删除的内容

This action does not delete your Google, LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram account. You can revoke AUTOLINK access separately in the provider’s account settings. / 此操作不会删除你的 Google、LinkedIn、Facebook 或 Instagram 账号。你也可以在相应平台的账号设置中撤销 AUTOLINK 授权。
